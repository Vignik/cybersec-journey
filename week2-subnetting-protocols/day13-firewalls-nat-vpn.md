# Day 13: Firewalls, NAT, and VPNs

## 📖 Topic
Three core technologies for controlling and protecting network traffic.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- **Firewall**: a system that filters incoming/outgoing traffic based on
  rules (allow/block by IP, port, protocol).
  *Example: a firewall rule blocking all inbound traffic on port 23
  (Telnet) because it's an old, insecure protocol.*

- **NAT (Network Address Translation)**: translates private IPs to a
  public IP (and back) so multiple devices on a LAN can share one public
  IP address to reach the internet.
  *Example: your home router uses NAT so your laptop (`192.168.1.15`) and
  phone (`192.168.1.20`) both appear as your single public IP to the
  outside internet.*

- **VPN (Virtual Private Network)**: creates an encrypted tunnel between
  your device and a VPN server, hiding your traffic from your local
  network/ISP and often masking your real IP.
  *Example: using a VPN on public Wi-Fi so someone on the same network
  can't snoop on your traffic.*
